Book one, or line them up in order: fill the cracks, seal the surface, then stripe it fresh.

Line striping

New layouts and restripes that are straight, bright and easy to read from the driver’s seat.

  • Parking stalls and new lot layouts
  • ADA accessible stalls and signage
  • Fire lanes and no-parking zones
  • Arrows, crosswalks, stop bars and stencils

Seal coating

A protective coat that shields asphalt from sun, water, oil and salt, and brings back that deep black finish.

  • Surface cleaning and oil-spot prep
  • Even, full-coverage application
  • Slows cracking and extends pavement life
  • Pairs with a restripe for a like-new lot

Crack filling

Sealing cracks keeps water out of the base, so small cracks don’t turn into potholes by spring.

  • Cracks cleaned out before filling
  • Flexible filler that moves with the pavement
  • Best done before seal coating
  • Cheaper than patching or repaving later
